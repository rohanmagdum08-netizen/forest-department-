ABOUT THE PLATFORM

One connected view of forest care.

ForestGuard 360 is a local demonstration platform for coordinated forest management and community stewardship.

Operational modules

Patrols, wildlife rescue workflows, fire reports, plantation monitoring, eco-tourism bookings, and human-wildlife conflict response.

Built for transparent action

Role-based permissions, recorded locations, rule-based fire risk, and capacity checks keep decisions traceable.

This software uses manually recorded sightings and sample data. It does not detect fires automatically or track animals continuously.
